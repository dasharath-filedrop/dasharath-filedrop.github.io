Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Dasharath File Drop is a private tool used by its owner to receive files from a trusted person into the owner's own Google Drive.

Data accessed: Only files and folders created by the tool itself in the owner's Google Drive (drive.file scope).

Data use and sharing: Files are stored only in the owner's Google Drive. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The tool does not collect analytics or personal data.

Revoking access: The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
